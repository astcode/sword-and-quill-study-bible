Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 27, 2026 · Sword & Quill Study Bible (iPadOS)

The short version: Sword & Quill Study Bible does not collect, track, sell, or transmit your personal data. Your Bible reading, highlights, Apple Pencil margin drawings, notebooks, stickers, and study trails stay 100% private on your iPad.

1. Zero Data Collection & Zero Tracking

  • No Accounts or Logins: You never have to create an account, enter an email address, or sign in to use Sword & Quill.
  • No Analytics or Telemetry: We do not embed third-party analytics SDKs, advertising frameworks, crash trackers, or behavioral tracking pixels.
  • 100% Offline Study Engine: Scripture, Strong’s Concordance, Hebrew/Greek interlinear, dictionaries, offline meaning search, handwriting OCR, and audio narration all run directly on your device.

2. Local Storage & Backups

All personal study data — including highlights, bookmarks, Sermon Notebooks, Apple Pencil drawings, custom stickers, and Study Trails — is stored locally on your iPad inside your app container (user_study.mql).

If you choose to export a backup file (.mql), a printable PDF, or a shared Study Lesson (.sqstudy / Classroom QR Code), those files are created locally on your device and shared only with the destinations you explicitly choose via Apple’s system Share Sheet or AirDrop.

3. Optional Permissions

  • Photo Library (Optional): Only accessed if you choose to import a photo into the Sticker Creator Studio. Imported images are stored locally on your device and never uploaded.
  • Apple Maps (MapKit): When viewing the interactive Biblical Places map, Apple’s native MapKit framework loads map tiles directly from Apple in accordance with Apple’s privacy policy. All 1,335 biblical place coordinates, descriptions, and nearby-place searches run 100% offline.

4. In-App Purchases (Apple StoreKit 2)

Optional purchases — including Classroom Sharing Pro and optional developer support tips — are processed exclusively by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit 2. We never receive or store your payment information, billing address, or Apple Account details.

5. Children’s Privacy

Because Sword & Quill collects no personal information from any user, it complies with children’s privacy standards (including COPPA) by design.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please email support@swordandquillbible.com.
