Support & Frequently Asked Questions

Need help with Sword & Quill Study Bible on your iPad? Email support@swordandquillbible.com.
How do I back up and restore my Apple Pencil notes, highlights, and stickers?

Open the top-left ☰ Menu → Study Dashboard & Backup and tap Export Backup (.mql). Save the single portable file to iCloud Drive, Files, or AirDrop it to another device. To restore on a new iPad, tap Restore from Backup and select your .mql (or legacy .sqlite) backup file — Sword & Quill automatically creates a safety snapshot first.

How does Classroom QR Code sharing work without internet?

With Classroom Sharing Pro, open your active Study Session and tap Share / QR. Sword & Quill compresses your lesson directly into a QR code or .sqstudy file with zero server involved. Students, spouses, or class members simply scan the QR code with their iPad camera (or accept the AirDrop file) to import the complete lesson for free.

Do my students or family members need to buy Classroom Pro to receive my lesson?

No! Receiving and importing .sqstudy packages, QR codes, and clickable links is 100% free for everyone. Only the teacher or leader exporting lessons needs the one-time Classroom Pro unlock.

How do I keep a sermon series separate from my everyday Bible notes?

Tap the top-right Study Trail icon and create a New Study. While a Study Session is active, your Apple Pencil ink, pinned verses, clips, and stickers live on their own isolated “tracing-paper” layer. Exiting the Study returns you to a clean everyday Bible — except any permanent Study Link badges you explicitly pin to your Bible.

How do I get a more natural voice for Offline Read-Aloud Audio?

On your iPad, open Settings → Accessibility → Read & Speak → Voices → English and download any free Apple Enhanced or Premium voice (such as Zoe Premium or Daniel Enhanced). Then select it inside Sword & Quill’s audio mini-bar.

How do I restore my Classroom Pro purchase on a new iPad?

Open ☰ Menu → Support & Classroom Pro and tap Restore Purchases while signed into the same Apple Account you originally purchased with.
